# Dozy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 12, 2026_

Dozy ("the app," "we," "us") is a sleep and nap companion that reads your
sleep data from Apple Health to estimate sleep debt, recommend nap windows,
and measure restorative sleep. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains
what data Dozy uses, how it is handled, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Dozy reads your sleep data **on your device only**.
- Dozy does **not** send your health data to any server, and we do not have
  access to it.
- Dozy does **not** sell, share, or use your health data for advertising.
- The only data that leaves your device is what Apple Health itself syncs
  across your own devices through your iCloud account, under Apple's control.

## Health data we read

With your permission, Dozy reads the following from Apple Health:

- **Sleep Analysis** — your recorded sleep sessions and sleep stages
  (light, deep, REM). Dozy uses this to calculate sleep debt, your restorative
  sleep percentage, and a personalized nap recommendation.

Dozy requests this access through Apple's HealthKit permission prompt. You can
grant or deny it, and you can change your choice at any time in
Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Dozy.

## Health data we write

With your permission, Dozy writes the following to Apple Health:

- **Sleep Analysis** — when you complete a nap using Dozy's nap timer, Dozy
  logs that nap as sleep so it counts toward your recovery and stays consistent
  across your devices.

Dozy only writes naps you actively start and complete in the app. You can
review or delete this data at any time in the Apple Health app under the
Dozy source.

## How your data is used

All calculations — sleep debt, nap recommendations, and restorative sleep —
happen **locally on your device**. Your health data is never transmitted to us
or to any third party. We do not operate servers that receive your health
information.

## Account information

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Dozy stores the identifier Apple provides,
along with your name and email if you choose to share them, **locally on your
device** to maintain your session. This information is not transmitted to us.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your health data or personal information
with anyone. Dozy contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that
access your health data.

## Children's privacy

Dozy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this
page with an updated date.
