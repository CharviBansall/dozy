# Dozy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Dozy ("the app," "we," "us") turns your sleep data into a daily plan: how much
sleep you owe, and the one thing to do about it today. This policy explains
what data Dozy uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The same policy is published at <https://www.appdozy.com/privacy>.

## The short version

- Sleep and heart data from **Apple Health stays on your device**. Dozy never
  uploads it.
- Signing in sends a sign-in token from Apple or Google to our server, so we
  can confirm it is you. We do not keep a database of accounts.
- If you choose to connect **Oura or Garmin**, your sleep summaries from that
  tracker pass through our server and our data partner, Junction.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time: **You → Delete Account**.

## Apple Health data

With your permission, Dozy reads **Sleep Analysis** from Apple Health: your
recorded sleep sessions and sleep stages (core, deep, REM). Dozy uses it to
work out your sleep need, your sleep debt, tonight's bedtime, and whether a nap
is worth taking.

With your permission, Dozy also reads your **heart rate, heart rate variability
and breathing rate** for the hours you were asleep. It shows them beside your
deep and REM sleep and compares them with your own usual.

With your permission, Dozy also writes to Apple Health: when you finish a nap
with Dozy's nap timer, the nap is saved as sleep so it counts toward your debt.
Dozy only writes naps you start and complete in the app.

All of these calculations happen **on your device**. Apple Health data is not
sent to our server or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising. If you
use iCloud for Health, Apple syncs that data between your own devices under
Apple's control.

You can change Dozy's access at any time in
Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Dozy, and you can review or delete
the naps Dozy wrote in the Health app.

## Your account

Dozy asks you to sign in with Apple or with Google.

- **What is sent to us.** The app sends the sign-in token issued by Apple or
  Google to our server at appdozy.com. The server checks the token and returns
  a session that lasts about 90 days. The token contains an account identifier
  and may contain your email address.
- **What we keep.** We do not store your name, email address, or the token.
  The server keeps no list of accounts; the session is held on your phone, in
  the iOS Keychain.
- **What stays on your phone.** Your name and email, if Apple or Google share
  them, are stored only on your device so the app can show them to you.

Apple and Google each process the sign-in under their own privacy policies.

## Oura and Garmin (optional)

You can use Dozy with Apple Health alone. If you choose to connect an Oura or
Garmin account in **You → Sleep Sources**:

- You sign in to that service through **Junction** (Junction Technologies,
  formerly Vital), a health-data provider we use to connect trackers.
- Junction retrieves your **sleep summaries** from that service: dates, bed and
  wake times, and time in each sleep stage. It stores them on our behalf.
- Our server requests those summaries from Junction and passes them to the app
  on your phone. The server stores the identifier that links your Dozy account
  to Junction, and nothing else about your sleep.

We use this data only to calculate your plan. It is not sold, shared for
advertising, or used to train models.

## Other services we rely on

- **Vercel** hosts our website and server. Like any web host, it logs
  technical details of requests, such as IP address and time.
- **Stripe** processes payments if you buy a subscription on our website. We
  never see your full card number. Purchases made inside the iPhone app, when
  available, are handled by Apple.

Dozy contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

## Notifications

If you turn them on, nap alarms and the morning summary are created by the app
on your device. Their content is not sent to us.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Delete your account** in the app under You → Delete Account. This signs you
  out, clears Dozy's data from your phone, disconnects any Oura or Garmin
  account, and tells Junction to delete the sleep data it holds for you.
  Junction disconnects trackers immediately and erases the data within about
  seven days.
- **Sign out** removes your session from the phone. A connected tracker stays
  linked on our side until you delete your account.
- Sleep data in Apple Health belongs to you and is not affected. You can remove
  it in the Health app.
- You can also email us to ask for deletion, or to ask what we hold about you.

## Children

Dozy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.
